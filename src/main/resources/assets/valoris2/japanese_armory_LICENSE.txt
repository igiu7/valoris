The Japanese armor and weapons of Valoris (registry/JapaneseArmory.java, client/JapaneseArmorModels.java and the
textures and models under textures/item/japanese, textures/block/japanese, models/item/japanese and
textures/models/armor/japanese_*) are a port to Minecraft 1.21.1 of

    Epic Knights : Japanese Armory 1.6.2 by chivas477
    https://legacy.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/epic-knights-japanese-armory

licensed under the Academic Free License v3.0 (https://opensource.org/license/afl-3-0-php).

Changes: rewritten for NeoForge 1.21.1, items moved to the valoris2 namespace (some renamed: kitsune_mask,
oni_mask, iron_jutte, iron_sasumata, wooden_*, japanese_*), made at the Armory Table instead of crafting recipes,
armor and damage balanced by era, cloth pieces turned into decorations.
